Beauty tips

Prep your skin before makeup

Cleanse, moisturise and use sunscreen so foundation sits smoothly and lasts through long events.

Protect your hair between styles

Moisturise often, sleep in a satin bonnet and avoid tight styles that pull on your edges.

Choosing a bridal look

Book a trial early, bring inspiration photos and plan for photography lighting.
